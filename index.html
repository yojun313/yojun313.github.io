About

I study Computer Science and Engineering at POSTECH. Since my first year I have been building research software for the Korean National Police University. What began as a crawler is now a set of web services, two servers that I run and a local LLM the whole lab uses.

I like building things people actually open every day, and I keep improving them after launch. Most of the projects below are live. I also make tools for my own work; UnivDash is where I do all of my development now.

2026

Plate Masking

masking.yojun.devModel on Hugging Face

Masks license plates in dashcam video. Vehicles are found first and plates are searched inside each vehicle, so small, distant plates are not missed. I fine-tuned a pretrained YOLO11x plate detector with downscaling, blur, noise and compression, then added optical-flow tracking and parallel video processing.

99.0% recall and 0.98 F1 on the image evaluation set, 27+ fps on the development GPU

2026

Forgery Detection

forge.yojun.dev

Finds the regions of a claim document that were edited with generative AI and scores the whole document. Built with another intern; I led the model experiments. We made FinForge, a 6,000-image dataset, and fine-tuned TruFor on it.

Localization F1 from 0.51 to 0.97, test accuracy from 55% to 93%
